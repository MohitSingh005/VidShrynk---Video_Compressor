Absolutely private. VidShrynk runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript and the Canvas API. Your video bytes never leave your computer. We don't have servers that touch your files.
Input: MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, WebM, WMV, and any format your browser's native video decoder supports. Output: MP4 (H.264 via MediaRecorder) or WebM. More output formats are on our roadmap.
There's no hard server limit since nothing is uploaded. The practical limit depends on your device's RAM. Most modern computers handle files up to several GB comfortably. Very large files (10GB+) may be slow depending on your hardware.
VidShrynk uses the browser's native MediaRecorder API combined with Canvas rendering to re-encode video frames at reduced quality and resolution. This is fast, private, and requires no plugins or downloads. For deeper codec control, a future WebAssembly FFmpeg mode is planned.
Compression speed depends on video length, resolution, and your device's CPU. A 1-hour 4K video will take longer than a 2-minute 720p clip. Reducing resolution scale to 50% or lower dramatically speeds up processing.
