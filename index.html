CRF (Constant Rate Factor) is the quality scale FFmpeg uses. Lower = better quality and larger file (10 is near lossless). Higher = smaller file with more compression (51 is lowest quality). 23 is a great default for most uses.
Absolutely. FFmpeg.wasm runs entirely inside your browser using WebAssembly. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Your files never leave your computer.
